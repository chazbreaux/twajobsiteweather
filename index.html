About this data

Each day’s record fills in as it happens, at about 08:00, 12:00 and 17:00 Central time, and only for sites marked for history. Overnight rain covers the previous day from 17:01 to 08:00. Conditions are the average cloud cover for the period (Sunny, Partly cloudy or Cloudy), or Rain if any rain fell during it. Rain amounts and conditions come from hourly weather model data for each site’s location, not from a rain gauge, so they are estimates. Warnings and watches come from the National Weather Service and refresh every 2 minutes. Storm tracks come from the National Weather Service radar storm-tracking data (via Iowa State’s Mesonet) and mark radar-detected rotation and its projected path; a rotation or tornado-signature flag is a radar indication, not a confirmed tornado, and some storms are missed or false-alarmed. All of this is a convenience, not a substitute for official alerts, so always follow local emergency instructions. The page refreshes itself, so it can stay open on a screen.
