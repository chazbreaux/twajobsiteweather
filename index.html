About this data

Each day’s record fills in as it happens, at about 08:00, 12:00 and 17:00 Central time, and only for sites marked for history. Overnight rain covers the previous day from 17:01 to 08:00. Conditions are the average cloud cover for the period (Sunny, Partly cloudy or Cloudy), or Rain if any rain fell during it. Rain amounts and conditions come from hourly weather model data for each site’s location, not from a rain gauge, so they are estimates. Warnings and watches come from the National Weather Service and refresh every 5 minutes; they are a convenience, not a substitute for official alerts, so always follow local emergency instructions. The page refreshes itself, so it can stay open on a screen.
